District 214 Accounting | Chapter 5: Inventory Costing

Same goods, three costs.

FIFO

Oldest units go out first. Sales pull from the top of the list; the newest costs stay on hand.

LIFO

Newest units on hand go out first. Only layers already bought on the sale date count.

Weighted Avg

One pool. Recompute the average after every purchase; sales come out at the current average.
